A likelihood by impact risk matrix. By default, the colour of a cell is its risk level, from its position in the two scales: green, amber, or red. The number in a cell is the number of risks in it. Cells without risks also show their colour.

== Parameters

* `title` (required): the title above the matrix.
* `legend`: the text below the matrix.
* `likelihood` (required): the names of the likelihood levels, lowest first. Three to five levels. The number of levels sets the height of the matrix.
* `impact` (required): the names of the impact levels, lowest first. Three to five levels. The number of levels sets the width of the matrix.
* `likelihoodTitle` and `impactTitle`: the titles of the axes. The defaults are "Likelihood" and "Impact".
* `cells`: the number of risks in each cell. Each cell has a `likelihood` and an `impact`, as positions in the scales (1 is the lowest), and a `count`.

== Risk levels of your own method

If your risk method gives a risk level to each cell, give `levels` and `levelGrid` together. The colours of the matrix then follow your method, and a legend lists the levels.

* `levels`: the risk levels, lowest first, two to five. Each level has a `name` and a `colour`, a step of the colour scale: 1 green, 2 light green, 3 amber, 4 red, or 5 dark red.
* `levelGrid`: the risk level of each cell. One row for each likelihood level, lowest first. Each row has one entry for each impact level, lowest first. Each entry is a position in `levels`, where 1 is the first level.
