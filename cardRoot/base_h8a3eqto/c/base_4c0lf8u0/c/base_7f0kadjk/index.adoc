= Steps widget

The widget numbers the steps. A done step has a tick, the next step is highlighted, and an open step is dimmed. A step with a card key opens that card in the app.

== First steps of a project, with links

The fifth step opens the progress tiles test page.

{{#report}}
  "name": "base/reports/stepsWidget",
  "title": "First steps",
  "legend": "Four of eight steps done",
  "nextLabel": "your next step",
  "steps": [
    {"label": "Describe the product and set the owner", "status": "done"},
    {"label": "Assign people to security roles", "status": "done"},
    {"label": "Check the competence of the team", "status": "done"},
    {"label": "Document the security context", "status": "done"},
    {"label": "Make a threat model", "status": "next", "cardKey": "base_ybuxq82o"},
    {"label": "List the high-level risks", "status": "open"},
    {"label": "Write security requirements", "status": "open"},
    {"label": "Choose the practices that you need", "status": "open"}
  ]
{{/report}}

== Long labels

The widget fits the content column of the app at 1280 pixels. A long label breaks into two lines. A label that is longer than two lines ends with an ellipsis, and the tooltip shows the full label.

{{#report}}
  "name": "base/reports/stepsWidget",
  "title": "Set up the home of the information security management system",
  "nextLabel": "your next step",
  "steps": [
    {"label": "Give the home a title that names the scope, for example the organisation, a business unit, or one team", "status": "done"},
    {"label": "Set the owner of the home and answer the questions about the scope of the information security management system", "status": "next"},
    {"label": "Add the libraries that the answers need", "status": "open"},
    {"label": "Read the guidance of each library, assign the owners of the registers, and plan the first reviews of the controls together with the security team and the management of the organisation", "status": "open"}
  ]
{{/report}}
