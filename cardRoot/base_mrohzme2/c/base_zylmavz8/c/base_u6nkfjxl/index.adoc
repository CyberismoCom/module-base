Base has one-time tasks and recurring tasks.

* A task (`base/cardTypes/task`, template "Task") is done once. It can have a due date.
* A monthly, quarterly, or annual task recurs. Make it from the template "Monthly task", "Quarterly task", or "Annual task". It has the field "Last reviewed". The template sets the review period: 30 days for a monthly task, 90 days for a quarterly task, and 365 days for an annual task.

The workflow states of a one-time task are Open, In progress, Closed as done, and Closed as not applicable. A recurring task also has the state To do. Its transition "Review as OK" records a new review of a task that is closed as done.

== What the rules check

* A task that is not closed fails the policy check "Due date passed" when its due date has passed.
* To close a task as not applicable ("Review as not applicable"), fill in the field "Justification" first. Without a justification, the transition is denied.
* A closed recurring task without a "Last reviewed" date fails the policy check "Date of latest review missing".
* A closed task fails the policy check "Review expired" when more days have passed since "Last reviewed" than its review period allows.

The transitions "Done", "Review as OK", "Review as applicable", and "Review as not applicable" of a recurring task set "Last reviewed" to the current date.

== Adoption steps

The field "Adoption step" (`base/fieldTypes/adoptionStep`) puts the work of a module in order. Its values are Step 1, Step 2, and Step 3. The predicate `base_greaterAdoptionStep` compares a card with its ancestors and descendants. A card on a higher step is greater, and a card with a step is greater than a card without one.
