== Decisions

A decision (`base/cardTypes/decision`, template "Decision") records a decision, its context, and its consequences. It has an owner and a decision date. Its workflow states are Draft, Approved, and Deprecated. The link type "Supersedes" links a decision to the decision that it replaces.

== Meeting memos

A meeting memo (template "Meeting memo") is a page with sections for the meeting details, the goals, the notes, and the action points. A button under "Action points" makes a task under the memo, and a table lists the tasks. When you create a meeting memo, base adds the current date to the start of its title.

== Role assignments

A role assignment (`base/cardTypes/roleAssignment`, template "Role assignment") assigns a person to a role. The title is the name of the role, and the owner is the person. Its workflow states are Draft, Assigned, and Archived.

The report "Role assignments" (`base/reports/roleAssignments`) shows the role assignments under a card in a table. Archived assignments are in a separate table.

== Pages

A page (`base/cardTypes/page`, template "Page") is a general content page. Its workflow states are Draft, Ready, and Deprecated. The reports "Children list" (`base/reports/childrenList`) and "Table of child cards" (`base/reports/childrenTable`) list the cards under a page.
